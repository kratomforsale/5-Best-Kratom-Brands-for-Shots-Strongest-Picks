The extraction process matters enormously.

Kratom alkaloids — primarily mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine — must be extracted from plant material into a liquid matrix through a process that preserves alkaloid integrity while concentrating them to meaningful per-bottle dosing levels.

A 2016 study in the Journal of Medicinal Chemistry (Váradi et al., 2016) confirmed that 7-hydroxymitragynine's potency is directly dependent on its molecular integrity — meaning extraction methods that damage the alkaloid's chemical structure produce a product that tests positive for the compound but delivers diminished pharmacological activity.

Thermal stability is the second critical variable.

Premium shot manufacturers use low-temperature extraction protocols and alkaloid stabilization additives — typically antioxidant compounds — to protect active content through the manufacturing process.

Budget manufacturers heat-extract at higher temperatures for faster production throughput.

Kratom alkaloids are lipophilic — they absorb more completely in the presence of lipid carrier compounds.

Premium shot manufacturers incorporate lipid carrier systems or bioavailability-enhancing compounds into their formulations.
